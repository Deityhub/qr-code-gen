Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no account and has no scan limits or expiry. The QR codes you download are static and never depend on this website.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The QR code is generated entirely in your browser, so the text or link you enter never leaves your device.

Should I download SVG or PNG?

Use SVG for print and anything that must scale without losing sharpness. Use PNG for web pages, slides and chat apps.

Which error correction level should I choose?

M (15%) suits most uses. Choose Q or H if the code may be printed small, scuffed or have a logo placed over it; higher levels make the code denser.
